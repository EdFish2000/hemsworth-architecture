Practice

We build for longevity — in material, in performance, and in the relationship between a building and the people it serves.

Hemsworth Architecture was founded on the conviction that the most enduring buildings are those that respond honestly to their site, their programme, and the limits of what the natural world can absorb. Our work spans civic institutions, industrial facilities, and private residences, but a consistent thread runs through each project: a commitment to mass timber as a structural material that is at once deeply practical and irreducibly beautiful.

Cross-laminated timber and glulam are not aesthetic choices for us — they are structural and environmental ones. Wood sequesters carbon, performs predictably under load, and creates interior environments that research consistently links to human wellbeing. We pair this material thinking with high-performance envelope design: continuous insulation, carefully modelled thermal bridging, passive solar orientation, and mechanical systems scaled to what the building actually needs rather than what convention prescribes. The result is buildings that use a fraction of the energy of their counterparts while providing superior indoor comfort.

We are a Vancouver practice, which means we work with a particular climate, a particular landscape, and a set of communities whose relationship to the forest and the mountain is not abstract. Our projects tend to be site-specific in the literal sense — responsive to topography, prevailing wind, the angle of winter sun, the proximity of mature trees. We consider the building's relationship to the ground as carefully as we consider its relationship to the sky.

Community use and public benefit shape every brief we accept. Whether the project is a branch library in Burnaby or a private retreat in the mountains north of Squamish, we ask who else will be affected by this building, and how it can serve more than its immediate programme. Architecture at its most useful is infrastructure — it frames the conditions for connection, for quiet, for work, and for rest. We take that responsibility seriously.
