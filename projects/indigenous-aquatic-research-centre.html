The Indigenous Aquatic Research Centre is an Indigenous-led applied research and stewardship facility on the Campbell River waterfront, brought forward by the Wei Wai Kum First Nation in partnership with the Nanwakolas Council Society and the Ha'ma'yas Stewardship Network. Set on the site of a repatriated former cruise-ship terminal, the Centre supports First Nations leadership in the co-governance and management of the marine environment, creating a space for dialogue between Western science and Indigenous knowledge.

The Centre houses an ISO-accredited environmental diagnostic and analytics laboratory alongside spaces for training, gathering, and cultural continuity, including a welcoming hall, Elders' space, classroom, and daycare. Ceremonial spaces, carved totemic welcome figures, and a restored waterfront landscape root the building in place and community. Together, these spaces provide a platform for Indigenous peoples to lead, manage, and innovate in a shared environmental and economic future.
